NHL Goalie Fantasy Scoring —

Game-by-game goalie workload vs. fantasy score, built from MoneyPuck data. Each dot is one goalie-game.
FP = Saves − Goals Against + 10 × Shutout + 2 × Win
Every save is worth +1 point. Every goal allowed costs −1 point. A shutout (0 goals against) adds a +10 bonus. A win adds a small +2 bonus. Simply starting a game is worth 0 points on its own — a goalie can be pulled minutes into a start due to injury or a blowout, so a start by itself says nothing about performance. The scoring is built around workload and stops-per-shot, not the scoreline your team handed you.
ℹ Win/loss isn't in the MoneyPuck export, so it's derived: each team's goals-against total (summed across any goalies used that game) equals the opponent's goals scored, so comparing the two teams' totals for a gameId gives the winner without needing an external result feed. Games where both teams show equal goals went to a shootout — the shot-by-shot result isn't in this data, so those are marked "no decision" and score 0 win bonus either way.

Why this formula, not "wins"

The goal is to reward a goalie's actual workload and stop rate, not the team in front of him. Under this system, 18 saves, 1 GA, win scores 17 + 2 = 19, while 35 saves, 2 GA, loss scores 33 — the busier, better-performing goalie wins the comparison even though his team lost. Wins still count for something (+2), but they can't dominate the score the way they do in most standard scoring systems.

Scoring System A vs. System B — where they disagree

System B: 0.7 × Saves − 4 × Goals Against — editable in the panel above, win bonus included when set. Its default −4-per-goal penalty dominates the score, so it punishes a goalie for playing behind a bad defense far more harshly than it rewards him for facing a lot of shots. System A's default −1-per-goal weight lets high-workload, high-save performances score well even with several goals against. Each axis below is this game's percentile rank within its own system (0 = worst game of the season, 100 = best), so the two systems are compared on equal footing regardless of their different raw scales. Dots on the diagonal agree; dots far off it are games the two systems rank very differently.

NHL Skater Fantasy Scoring —

Game-by-game skater production vs. fantasy score, built from MoneyPuck data. Each dot is one skater-game.
FP = 6×G + 4×A + 2×(+/−) + 2×PPP + 0.9×SOG + 1×BLK
Both scoring systems below default to Yahoo Fantasy Hockey's own published defaults: System A is Yahoo's Head-to-Head Points default (Goals, Assists, +/−, PPP, SOG, Blocks), System B is Yahoo's Season Points default (Goals, Assists, +/−, PIM, PPP, SOG). Every weight is editable with a −10 to 10 slider, same as the goalie page. Game-Winning Goal is a Yahoo category but isn't computable here — this per-game aggregated data has no goal-by-goal sequencing, so it's locked at 0 on both systems rather than guessed. Plus/minus is approximated from 5-on-5 on-ice goals for vs. against (excludes true 4-on-4/3-on-3, which this export lumps into an "other" bucket).
